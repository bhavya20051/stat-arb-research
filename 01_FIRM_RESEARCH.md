## Our inference about a suitable student project (not a firm statement)

Across the eight sources the recurring elements are: statistical analysis of large market datasets; predictive models and signal/feature research; explicit backtesting and out-of-sample evaluation; execution and order-placement awareness; transaction-cost awareness; time-series methods and machine learning "from linear models to deep learning"; Python with Pandas/NumPy as the research language and C++ as the production language.

A market-neutral, short-horizon equity statistical-arbitrage study with a point-in-time universe, an explicit execution and cost model, pre-registered hypotheses, multiple-testing control, and one C++ component exercises every one of those elements. It does not require proprietary data or infrastructure, which is why it is the project family selected in `02_STRATEGY_SELECTION.md`.
