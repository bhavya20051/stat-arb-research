No primary-source statement was located quantifying their own access-fee, clearing, or borrow economics. Best secondary proxy: use Virtu's public 10-K ratio (item 22–23) as the only SEC-disclosed comparable for a similar-profile electronic market maker.
- **Self-clearing member's actual per-share DTCC/NSCC total cost** (as opposed to the published fee-schedule components in items 18–20): UNVERIFIED at the "typical all-in per share" level — NSCC's schedule prices clearance by dollar value of positions (items 18–19), not per share, so a firm-specific per-share equivalent depends on average trade size and netting ratio and cannot be derived from the public fee schedule alone.
- **Actual negotiated GC repo spread/haircut for a specific prime-broker or self-clearing arrangement**: UNVERIFIED — item 21 gives the market GC benchmark rate (BGCR), not a specific firm's borrow cost, which in practice trades at a spread to GC (typically small, single-digit bps, for large low-fee-to-borrow names) that is not publicly disclosed.
- **Nasdaq exact non-tier/best-tier dollar figures (items 14–16)**: sourced via WebFetch's HTML-table extraction of Rule 7018 rather than a directly-quotable static PDF (Nasdaq's price list is a dynamic HTML page); figures were independently reproduced twice with consistent values, but confidence is secondary/moderate rather than primary-document-quote-grade. ## Reply to User (Recap)

**Recommended per-share/rate parameters for the backtest cost model:**
- Auction fill fee (MOC/LOC): **$0.0008/share** (range $0.0007–$0.0011)
- Add-liquidity rebate: **$0.0025/share credit** (range $0.0012–$0.0031, excluding Retail-only outlier tiers)
- Take-liquidity fee: **$0.0029/share** (range $0.0013–$0.0030; bound above by the current $0.0030 Reg NMS access-fee cap)
- Clearing (NSCC): **$2.60 per $1,000,000 notional** (into-net $0.44 + out-of-net $2.16), not a fixed per-share number
- SEC Section 31: **$0.0000206 per $1 of sale proceeds** (i.e., $20.60/$1,000,000), sales-only
- FINRA TAF: **$0.000195/share**, sales-only, capped at $9.79/trade
- GC borrow rate: **≈3.64% annualized** (BGCR, Sept 3, 2026)

**Three most important caveats:**
1. **The Reg NMS access-fee cap cut (to $0.0010/share) is adopted law but not yet in force.** The SEC has repeatedly deferred compliance (most recently to the first business day of November 2027 per Release 34-105656, June 11, 2026). Do not model the lower cap for any period before that date — the current, binding cap is still $0.0030/share.
2. **Neither Jane Street nor Citadel Securities discloses firm-specific per-share cost data.** All fee-schedule figures above are the public rate cards for NYSE/Nasdaq/NSCC/FINRA/SEC that any qualifying member pays; actual realized costs for a specific large market maker depend on their exact tier qualification (not disclosed) and could differ from the tiers assumed here. Virtu's 10-K ratio (≈31.6% of trading income, net, spent on brokerage/exchange/clearance fees and PFOF combined) is the only SEC-mandated, apples-to-comparable public disclosure found, and it bundles several cost types together (including retail PFOF payments Virtu itself makes as a wholesaler, which is not analogous to a pure prop-trading cost model).
3. **NSCC clearing fees are priced per dollar of notional (not per share)**, so any per-share clearing assumption in the backtest should instead be modeled as a small basis-point-of-notional charge (≈0.00026% combined into/out-of-net, i.e. $2.60 per $1,000,000) to remain price-level-invariant; a fixed per-share clearing assumption will misstate costs across the stock universe by average price.
