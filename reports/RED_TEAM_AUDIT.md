Kill-test suite (costs ×1.5/×2, +5 bp, lag, placebo, neighbours, universe subset, regimes) is the right list.

9. **Chronological splits with a 10-trading-day gap between windows**, no K-fold anywhere.

## 3. Independent classification

**D — invalid backtest as an instrument for the positive claims; the substantive negative conclusion stands and, once repaired, the study is a C.**

Reasoning:

- The two numbers that drove the project past DEV and VAL (0.89, 0.93) do not measure what the documents say they measure. Roughly half of the DEV+VAL gross P&L is Σ net_exposure × next-day SPY return generated by asymmetric LOC fills with an unexecuted hedge (F1); the DEV window is 2005–2011 in substance, below the pre-registered coverage threshold and carrying a 6–16% survivorship gap that removes exactly the 2008 failures a reversal book buys (F2, F5); the eligibility mask uses end-of-day information (F3); and "LOC" execution is 20% LOC with a 37% unconditional-MOC fallback (F4). Applying the project's own pre-registered DEV rule (start 2014) flips the DEV verdict from PASS to FAIL. A backtest whose in-sample verdict flips under its own rules, and whose Sharpe is half an unintended market-timing bet, is not a valid instrument.
- The holdout result (0.16, CI −1.06..1.46, ex-best-day 0.01) is *not* rescued by any of the defects: the leak was neutral-to-negative in 2023–2026, the market component was negative, and even the residual (market-component-removed) net SR is ≈0.5 with a CI spanning zero. The event and drift families are at zero. So the direction of the conclusion — no credible, economically meaningful edge at this horizon in this universe — is correct.
- The gap between D and C is not the outcome but the narrative: FINAL_DECISION attributes the failure to "decay of the gross edge in a calm regime" and says "the failure is not a cost or parameter artefact" and "VAL confirmed DEV almost exactly". The data say the net edge was ≈0 from 2012 onward inside the DEV window itself, the earlier "edge" was substantially construction-induced market exposure on a survivorship-affected panel, and VAL's 0.93 is two years of 2020–2021 (ex-2020–21: −0.82). Those are artefacts, and a recruiter-facing report that says "leakage-free", "beta-neutral", "delisting rules applied" is overclaiming on all three.
- Nothing found could turn this into B or A. Repairs (execute the hedge MOC, ex-ante jump mask, pre-registered DEV window, entries against held weights, delisting wiring, corrected dividend basis, honest era decomposition) would leave a clean research-quality negative with a credible mechanism story (day-one, no-news, stress-regime liquidity provision), which is a C worth presenting.
