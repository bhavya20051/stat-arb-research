# Résumé bullets (metrics only from the frozen analysis; nothing invented)

1. Built a Python/C++ statistical-arbitrage research system (point-in-time S&P 500 universe with delisted names, 15-minute intraday bars 2005–2026, SEC 8-K event layer timestamped to the second) and ran a pre-registered, locked-holdout test of residual short-term reversal across 140 registered configurations and five strategy variants; validation net Sharpe 0.93 (2019–2022) did not persist on the locked 2023–2026 holdout (net 0.16, gross 0.26), and the project reports a research-quality negative result with full cost, capacity and multiple-testing accounting.

2. Designed a leakage-free closing-auction execution model (signal at 15:40 ET from intraday bars, market-on-close / limit-on-close fills, exchange-member fee schedule from primary sources, Almgren impact on closing-auction volume) and showed the no-news reversal premium is a day-one, high-VIX phenomenon (development gross Sharpe ≈1.0, net 0.86 after costs; 6.8 bp/day no-news decile spread vs −15 bp/day continuation on earnings-8-K days).

3. Implemented the research infrastructure a quant desk expects: hypothesis registry with trial-type accounting feeding a Deflated Sharpe Ratio and CSCV probability of backtest overfitting (PBO 0.42), a 22-variant kill-test suite (cost stress, execution delay, parameter neighbours, placebo, regime and concentration cuts), a capacity curve by cost profile ($1M–$200M), 42 unit tests including hand-computed P&L and halt-then-delist cases, and a C++ simulation engine (ziglang, ctypes) with 1e-9 parity and a 6.7× speed-up.
